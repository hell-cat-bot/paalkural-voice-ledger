## LinkedIn

Most voice demos stop at a transcript. A milk collection line cannot — a wrong entry is money paid for milk that was never poured.

I built PaalKural (பால்குரல்): a Tamil/Tanglish voice ledger for a milk society. Every pour is decoded twice — raw and after a band+gate front-end. Both parses are compiled into member, litres, fat and shift, then validated. If the two listens agree, the sheet pre-fills. If they split, the entry is stamped CHECK and blocked until the operator resolves it. Nothing is written without a Tamil readback, a confirmation, and a receipt that keeps the heard transcript and an audit hash.

The honest part: on our own 24 synthetic clips, the filter did not consistently beat no filter at 5 dB (11/24 vs 12/24 exact; 24 synthetic clips, our benchmark). So the product reports the negative result and ships the gate instead of selling the filter. Then I reran the same matrix through Gnani Prisma live: 192 calls, 0 errors, and the gate pre-filled 20/24 clean, 17/24 at 15 dB, 16/24 at 5 dB and 12/24 at 0 dB with 0 silent errors in every condition. The app keeps the frozen local book and the live provider book separate, each with its own claim and 95% CIs.

72-second demo, code and both benchmark artifacts in the comments.
Synthetic voices and pours only. The live UI, video and provider check run on Gnani Prisma v3.0 and Timbre v2.5 through a participant-supplied key; the frozen benchmark stays local so it can be reproduced.
