## m00 · Title

Every pour on a milk line is money.
A wrong entry pays for milk that was never poured.
PaalKural listens twice, and writes once — or refuses.

## m01 · The idea

A transcript is not an entry.
So PaalKural decodes the same audio twice: once raw, and once after a band-limit and a spectral gate.
A deterministic compiler turns each transcript into four facts: member, litres, fat, and shift.
Both listens are validated against the society roster and the allowed ranges.
If both agree, the sheet pre-fills.
If only one validates, it pre-fills from that one.
If both validate but disagree, it stamps CHECK and blocks the log.

## m1 · Both listens agree

This is a five-decibel line: a fan, clinking cans, people talking.
Both listens return the same four values: member 127, three litres, fat 4.4, evening shift.
The sheet pre-fills, and the operator hears it read back in Tamil.
The operator confirms, and one entry lands with a receipt and an audit hash.

## m2 · Listens disagree

Now a harder clip.
The raw listen hears fat 4.7; the conditioned listen hears 4.0.
Both are valid values, and they disagree, so the sheet stamps CHECK and blocks the log.
The operator resolves it on the raw value, and only then does the entry write.
A silent disagreement becomes a visible decision.

## m4 · The day book

Every confirmed pour lands in the day book: receipt, member, litres, fat, rate, amount, audit hash, and the provider that heard it.
The society can export the whole day as a CSV for its accounts.

## m3 · The evidence

The Evidence Lab keeps two separate books.
Book one is our frozen local benchmark, and it reports a negative result: the filtered front end did not consistently beat raw audio.
So the product ships two listens and a gate, not a magic filter.
Book two reruns the same matrix live through Gnani Prisma: 192 calls, zero errors, and zero silent errors in every noise condition, across 24 synthetic clips, our benchmark.
The gate, not the filter, is the claim.

## m5 · Close

This is a complete product: an operator console, a write-blocking gate, receipts, a CSV day book, a live provider check, and 26 automated tests.
Every voice, name, and pour in this demo is synthetic.
Prisma and Timbre are wired live through a participant-supplied key.
PaalKural: one confirmed entry, or none.
